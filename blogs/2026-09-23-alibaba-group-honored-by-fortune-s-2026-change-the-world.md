Alibaba Group has been named to Fortune’s 2026 Change the World list for expanding AI access for the underserved consumers and SMEs, marking its eighth appearance on the list. The recognition reflects the company’s long-standing approach to building technology around practical problems. For Alibaba, AI is not a competitive race; it is a responsibility.
